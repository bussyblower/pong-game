# Overwatch Arena

A browser-based Overwatch-inspired arena shooter.

## Play Online

Visit: **https://bussyblower.github.io/pong-game/**

## Controls

- **Move**: W/A/S/D or Arrow keys
- **Aim**: Mouse
- **Shoot**: Left mouse click
- **Dash**: Space bar

## How to Play

Eliminate enemies, capture the objective, and survive against the red team. Manage your energy for abilities and shooting.
